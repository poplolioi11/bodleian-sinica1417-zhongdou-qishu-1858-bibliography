# 𠸄咭唎國新出種痘奇書 / Ying ji li guo xin chu zhong dou qi shu [A new treatise on vaccination from England]: Sinica 1417 (1858), bibliographic record

《𠸄咭唎國新出種痘奇書》（Ying ji li guo xin chu zhong dou qi shu）是牛津博德利圖書館藏、館目記為1858年於[香港]刊行並由Wilhelm Lobscheid編輯的中文種痘文獻（Sinica 1417）。本書目資料集整理題名異體、編者、刊年刊地、冊葉著錄，以及原件與IIIF影像的穩定連結，供晚清醫學出版、翻譯與文獻流通史研究定位此版本。提供JSON、CSV、TXT及TEI XML書目資料與中英文說明；未轉錄原書正文。資料由AI依館方書目及數位影像清單協助整理，尚未經人類專家審定；方括號刊地與題名首字的證據界線保留於來源及方法說明。

Ying ji li guo xin chu zhong dou qi shu (𠸄咭唎國新出種痘奇書) is a Chinese vaccination publication held by the Bodleian Library, Oxford, catalogued as an 1858 [Hong Kong] edition edited by Wilhelm Lobscheid (Sinica 1417). This bibliographic dataset identifies its title variants, editor, publication date and place, catalogued extent, and stable links to the original item and IIIF images, for research on late Qing medical publishing, translation, and circulation. It supplies JSON, CSV, TXT, and TEI XML bibliographic records with Chinese and English documentation; it does not transcribe the book. The records were compiled with AI assistance from the library catalogue and digital image inventory and have not been reviewed by a human subject specialist; the bracketed place and first title character retain their source qualifications.

## 檔案與用途

- `record.txt`：可直接閱讀的書目說明與來源。
- `record.json`：書目欄位、來源、證據限定與權利分層，供資料整理與查詢。
- `record.csv`：每列一項書目事實，附來源代碼與限制，適合試算表及資料庫。
- `record.tei.xml`：以TEI編碼書目與說明，便於與文獻資料交換；不是原書全文TEI。
- `SOURCES.md`：原件、館目、IIIF及研究文獻。
- `METHODS.md`：整理方法、證據層級與品質狀態。
- `CITATION.cff`：資料整理者與引用資訊。

原書中文為文言；本資料包的說明為繁體中文及英文。引用本包不等於引用完整原書轉錄。原書請循館藏號與原件連結另行引用。
