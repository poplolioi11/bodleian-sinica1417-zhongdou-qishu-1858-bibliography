# 𠸄咭唎國新出種痘奇書 · Sinica 1417

《𠸄咭唎國新出種痘奇書》（Ying ji li guo xin chu zhong dou qi shu；牛津博德利圖書館 Sinica 1417）是館目著錄由羅存德（Wilhelm Lobscheid）編輯、1858 年在［香港］重刻的漢文種痘小冊。原書敘述西方種痘法的傳入與流傳，附器具及痘形圖說、歷史論述與刊刻題署，可作晚清醫學出版、漢譯醫學及香港印刷史的文獻材料。本版首次提供這一館藏本14個主要原印文字面的影像初錄，另分列後加館藏文字及未歸屬折邊殘片，附逐單位來源定位、JSON、TXT與TEI XML。文字由AI輔助轉錄，疑讀與影像缺口已標記，尚未經人類全文校勘或專家審定。

Ying ji li guo xin chu zhong dou qi shu (𠸄咭唎國新出種痘奇書; Bodleian Libraries, Oxford, Sinica 1417) is a Chinese vaccination pamphlet catalogued as an 1858 [Hong Kong] reprint edited by Wilhelm Lobscheid. It discusses the introduction and circulation of a Western vaccination practice and includes historical explanations, instrument and lesion illustrations with textual labels, and publication statements. It provides source material for research on late Qing medical publishing, medical translation, and Hong Kong printing history. This version first supplies an image-based initial transcription of the witness’s 14 main original printed text faces, with later library inscriptions and unattributed folded-edge fragments distinguished, in source-linked JSON, TXT, and TEI XML. AI-assisted readings and image gaps are marked; the text has not undergone full human collation or subject-specialist review.
