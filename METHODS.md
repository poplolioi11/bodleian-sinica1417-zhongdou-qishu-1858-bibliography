# Methods and limits

The fixed official image sequence was inventoried in full before initial transcription. AI readers separately processed the first and second five canvases, keeping original text, diagrams’ textual labels, notes, running titles and later inscriptions in distinct units. The controller combined the two fixed outputs without changing their text strings, corrected delivery-field shapes and separated unattributed folded-edge fragments from the main text layer.

This v0.2.0 is an initial transcription, not the outcome of independent collation. Requested model settings are not runtime evidence; the exact native runtime model, effort and token counts are unknown and are not reported as zero. No human full-text double collation, expert adjudication, peer review or character-accuracy measurement has been performed. Native AI outputs are observations that may contain omissions and misreadings. Agreement, schema checks, checksums and DOI registration do not establish reading accuracy.

JSON is the fixed text source for deterministic TXT and TEI projections. String/order readback and TEI P5 4.12.0 Relax NG validation check structure and transfer, not image interpretation. This is a source-linked minimal TEI edition, not a complete critical apparatus or an ODD/Schematron-certified scholarly edition. Geometry is regional and approximate; no baselines or character boxes were invented.

Source scans, crops, dedicated software, prompts and full private work records are not distributed. The public package lets readers inspect the data, uncertainties and source links; it does not provide a complete image-to-output rerun method. The work is documentary medical history, with no biological research or modernization of historical procedures.
