# Reading guide

READABLE-TEXT.txt is the convenient reading derivative: adjacent body columns are concatenated within each source canvas and face, while title, diagram text and publication statements remain separate labelled blocks. Running titles, folio numbers, later inscriptions and unattributed edge remnants are omitted from that derivative only. No source words, punctuation or spelling are changed, and it is not an independently edited text. Editorial square-bracket labels are navigation, not words from the book. Retain book.json for full layers and source links.

book.txt is the complete unit-order view; its line breaks and [cNNN] headings are navigation, not source punctuation. It includes later inscriptions and fragments, so it is unsuitable for mechanically joining into prose. book.json supplies layer/role/face and source information; figure label order does not reconstruct an illustration. Open the official image to interpret the diagram.

The14 main printed faces are identified in scope.face_ids. Later exhibition/library text on c001 and other annotations belong to provenance. Unknown folded-edge text is kept as unassigned-fragment. A □ is an explicitly unresolved region: consult uncertainties.json for its extent and cause; do not replace it from another page’s familiar wording. Report a proposed correction with unit ID, official canvas URL and the visible supporting strokes at the GitHub Issues link in README.
