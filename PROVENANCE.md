# Source and edition

Shelfmark: Sinica 1417, Bodleian Libraries, University of Oxford. [Digital object](https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/objects/521aeb36-b21d-4536-bd16-d6157210aac3/); [catalogue](https://east-asian.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/catalog/volume_1133); [IIIF manifest](https://iiif.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/iiif/manifest/521aeb36-b21d-4536-bd16-d6157210aac3.json). The fixed catalogue XML is [Bodleian catalogue source](https://github.com/bodleian/east-asian-mss/blob/650ddc6cba758eeb892d2cacb25599bb23bd75ab/collections/chinese/Sinica%201417.xml).

The catalogue title is 英咭唎國新出種痘奇書; the source title is transcribed 𠸄咭唎國新出種痘奇書. Catalogue romanization is preserved as ying ji li guo xin chu zhong dou qi shu; its display form only capitalizes the first letter. The bracketed English title is an editorial translation.

The catalogue attributes editing of the1858 edition to Wilhelm Lobscheid. Literal historical responsibilities appear in c008-L. The printed earlier colophon 一千八百零四年 is retained separately from the reprint imprint 一千八百五十八年本港／憲諭重刻 atc009-R. The former does not date this physical witness. The supplied catalogue place [Hong Kong] and the printed 本港 are distinguished; composition date, exact printing technique and digital capture date have not been established here.

All10 official canvases were inventoried. The14 main printed faces are c002-L; c003-R/L throughc008-R/L; c009-R. c002-R andc009-L are blank inside covers; c010 is a blank lower cover. c001 carries later exhibition/library inscriptions. Eight narrow fragments visible at outer folded edges are preserved as unattributed source regions, with uncertain leaf identity and possible duplication.
